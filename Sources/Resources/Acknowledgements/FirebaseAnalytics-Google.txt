Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Moonlight uses Firebase Analytics to send anonymous usage statistics to Google
Analytics. Turn it off with the Google Analytics setting in Settings > Privacy.
Moonlight uses the FirebaseAnalyticsCore build, which does not include Google's
advertising-identifier or ad-conversion components.
